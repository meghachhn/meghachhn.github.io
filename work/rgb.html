RGB: A New Gender

March 2024

March 2024

After a surge and positive push towards raising kids as 'theybies' and studies constantly highlighting that gender identity begins to emerge between 18 months and the age of 3, many corporations and governments have decided to abolish the term gender and have introduced RGB system.

Gender identification so far has failed to capture the essence of people, the new system aims to capture and communicate our actions, our ways of thinking, the way we speak, and the way we function. These attributes now are being associated to a particular hex-code which determines our identity and the role we may play.

Megha Chauhan | 2026
